# Pre-design review (instructional protocol)
- What is the user's real question and what decision or outcome matters?
- Can the conclusion be given immediately, before visual detail?
- Is this a simple fact or a complex multi-constraint problem requiring decomposition, evidence, alternatives, and action?
- Would a magazine overview, relationship diagram, timeline, comparison, or interactive simulator materially improve understanding?
- Which claims need current/primary sources, counterevidence, and explicit uncertainty?
- For tool recommendations: is the version current and compatible with the user's actual environment? Does it duplicate native capabilities or create performance/maintenance costs?
- Can the benefits and limitations be shown together at the step or connection where they occur?
- Which interaction changes understanding or a decision, rather than adding decoration?
- What are the mobile constraints and text fallback if visuals are not displayed?
- Do not force a visualization or a follow-up question solely to satisfy a template.

This Markdown checklist is not an executable ChatGPT hook.
