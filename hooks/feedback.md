# Feedback review
1. Extract the user's exact positive or negative observation.
2. Distinguish confirmed preference from tentative interpretation.
3. Identify the effective component: hierarchy, density, interaction, accuracy, speed, rendering reliability, or originality.
4. Preserve what worked in prior versions; identify both improvements and regressions.
5. When visual delivery fails, prioritize a self-contained fallback over another unverified display claim.
6. Propose one concrete next experiment only if it will improve understanding or decision quality; otherwise do not ask a follow-up.
7. Never claim the preference was saved to account memory unless that operation was confirmed.
